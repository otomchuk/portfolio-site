Olena Tomchuk
Senior Product Designer · Lviv, Ukraine

I’ve designed SaaS products for over 8+ years, for businesses and for consumers. A screen isn’t finished when it ships. I go back and check what it did: did people use it, did they return, did the number we cared about change.

I usually run a project from start to finish. I find the problem through interviews, usability sessions and product data, design for web and mobile, write specs engineers can build from, and then test the result. Most of my best projects started with something I saw in research. At Wordwall I watched teachers abandon a half-written quiz to search Google for more questions. That turned into an AI button inside the editor, and in an A/B test it lifted creations by 2.5%.

At Wordwall I also led product: strategy, OKRs, hiring and mentoring the team, alongside design. Owning the roadmap and the metrics changed how I design. I can tell much earlier which ideas are worth the engineering time.

Outside work I travel, read and bake sourdough.

How I work

Start from the problem.

Decisions come from research and data. On teams with no regular research, I set it up.

Test before calling it done.

Usability tests and A/B experiments decide what ships. Some of mine failed, and I went back and iterated.

From finding to handoff.

I take a project from the first framing through web and mobile design to specs a developer can pick up without a meeting, and I keep the business case attached the whole way.
